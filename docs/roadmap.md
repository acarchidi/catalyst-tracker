# Roadmap

The build runs in twelve phases (0 to 11). Each phase ends at a gate, and the next phase does not start until the owner approves the gate. Week counts are estimates; the owner's review time sets the real pace.

Checkpoints inside each phase (where work stops for review) are listed in [quality-gates.md](quality-gates.md).

Claude Code: tick items (`[x]`) in the same pull request that finishes them.

## Phase 4 — Analysis (weeks 17–24)

- [ ] Readout benchmarking, oncology first. Compares a readout with competitors' programs in development and their published data, approved drugs, and the marketed standard of care where one exists; every row carries its comparability label, so loose comparisons are shown as loose (spec: Readout benchmarking; owner confirmed the scope 2026-10-17)
- [ ] Market-size estimates
- [ ] Pipeline and competitive-landscape views: one company's programs by indication and phase, and every company's programs in one indication or against one target, each with its next catalyst; as a table and as a chart (phase on one axis, programs grouped by indication), every point linked to its source (owner request, 2026-10-17)
- [ ] Catalyst scenarios (spec: Catalyst scenarios; owner decision 2026-10-17): for each upcoming catalyst, upside and downside outcome scenarios with estimated peak sales, risk-adjusted value as a share of market value, and a price-move range, every input shown; needs readout benchmarking, market-size estimates, and the price-data license first; publishes its accuracy record and a backtest on past catalysts at launch
- [ ] Collaborator exposure estimates (ADR 0028), with the catalyst scenarios: for a public collaborator, its disclosed share of the program (profit split, royalty, milestones) times the scenario's change in program value, as a share of its own market value; for a private one, the dollar value at stake, and its share of a publicly reported valuation where one exists; "terms not disclosed" when they are not; every input shown, labelled an estimate, with the not-financial-advice notice
- [ ] Reference ranges per trial measure (owner request, 2026-10-17): what result would likely read as negative, in line with the standard of care, or clearly positive, from benchmarks, labels, and published trials, each boundary linked to its sources; scenarios show where they fall
- [ ] Key publications on each catalyst record, as a field the viewer can hide (owner request, 2026-10-17):
  - Only peer-reviewed journal articles: PubMed records of type "journal article" in MEDLINE-indexed journals; no preprints, posters, company documents, or self-published material.
  - Linked to the company in one of two checkable ways: the article reports one of the company's registered trials (its NCT number is in PubMed's record), or an author lists the company as an affiliation. Funding statements are free text and too unreliable to use alone.
  - "Most important" ranked by NIH iCite's Relative Citation Ratio, which compares an article's citations with others in its field and year, so older articles do not win just by age; the top five shown, each linked to the journal's page.
  - Free sources with no new account: PubMed E-utilities and iCite.

## Phase 5 — Companies listed only outside the US

- [ ] Companies listed only outside the US, one market at a time in order of how many listed drug developers each has (owner request 2026-10-17; ADR 0021):
  - per market, before any code: robots.txt and terms checked, a rate limit set, and any account or key found (gathered for the owner in one sitting); a market whose terms forbid automated collection is left out or reached through a licensed feed the owner approves;
  - quotes kept in the original language, character for character, with a labeled translation beside them; gold-set entries in each language before that market publishes;
  - trial registries (EU CTIS and EudraCT, ISRCTN, jRCT, ChiCTR, ANZCTR, CRIS; WHO ICTRP as an index) and regulators (EMA, MHRA, PMDA, NMPA, Health Canada, TGA, Swissmedic, MFDS, Israel's Ministry of Health), each checked the same way;
  - ISIN and LEI codes and names in other scripts for the entity resolver;
  - companies already matched to an SEC number (through ISIN or LEI) are not added again;
  - a machine translation is labeled as one and is never a claim; date phrases in each language (including Japan's era years) get property-based tests;
  - no market value, price statistics, or scenario price ranges until a price license covers that exchange and currency;
  - checkpoint CP9 per market: a separate random sample of that market's records meets the Gate 2 error rate before it publishes.

## Phase 6 — Business agents (after v1; owner request 2026-10-17; ADR 0024)

- [ ] Competitor research agent, weekly: plain-code collectors keep dated copies of each competitor's public pricing, features, changelog, and release pages (list in `config/competitors.toml`); public launch and news feeds suggest new entrants for the owner to add; the agent reads only what changed since last week and writes a report in which every claim quotes the changed text and links the page, plus a ranked list of feature ideas (what, who it helps, who already does it, rough size); ideas become roadmap items only when the owner approves; each source's terms checked before it is added; plain code files the report as a weekly GitHub issue; its own `researcher` role and cap; checkpoint CP10
- [ ] CFO agent, monthly, read-only, drafts only: revenue from Stripe (restricted read-only key), AI spend from `model_call`, other costs, a monthly budget, and cash on hand from a file the owner updates monthly (`config/costs.toml`), no bank connection; totals only, never a subscriber's details; plain code computes revenue, costs, profit or loss, cost per subscriber, Stripe fees, churn, our own runway, and budget versus actual; the model only explains them, and a test checks every number it writes against the computed table; it may draft proposals (cap changes, cancelling an unused service, price or annual-plan changes) as issues for the owner, and can never pay, refund, change prices or caps, or move money; not tax advice; its own `cfo` role and cap; checkpoint CP10
- [ ] Feature suggestions (owner request 2026-10-18; ADR 0031): a short form on the website (1,000 characters; visitors rate-limited, with a spam trap; emails and phone numbers removed from the text); plain code drops spam and repeats, groups near-repeats, and counts who asked on which tier; a daily reviewer with no tools reads the groups as data beside the roadmap and spec and scores each one (already planned, forbidden, who it helps, effort, return 1 to 5); only new, allowed groups scoring 4 or 5 go to the owner in one daily GitHub issue, and nothing is sent on a day with none; the owner decides what becomes a roadmap item; its own `suggestions` role and cap

## Phase 7 — Insider purchases before catalysts (owner request 2026-10-17; ADR 0026)

- [ ] Insider purchases before catalysts, from SEC Form 4 filings (plain code, no model):
  - every insider transaction for covered companies, each with its filing link: name, role, date, code (P is a purchase), shares, price, value, and pre-set trading plan where marked;
  - for each upcoming catalyst, the purchases in the 90 days before its window opens (or since the company's previous catalyst, if shorter), with sales beside them for context;

  - later, once outcomes exist, a descriptive base rate with its sample size;
  - foreign companies' directors and officers from March 18, 2026, when their Form 4 filing began (check the SEC's exemption order first)

## Phase 9 — Alert channels, watchlist board, and the most-watched feed (owner request 2026-10-18; ADR 0029)

- [ ] Watchlist board: pin catalysts, companies, drugs, indications, and saved searches; a timeline that draws each date window at its real length (a dot for a day, a bar for a quarter), coloured by type, with its date label; zoom, grouping, filters, and an overlap view of how many watched catalysts fall in each week or month; slips shown as the old window faded with an arrow to the new one; refreshes after each run; a table view with the same data for accessibility; export as image and .ics; read-only sharing, off by default; no price series; free boards keep the free watchlist limit
- [ ] Most-watched and most-alerted ranking, counted in plain code after each run over 7 days: totals only, a catalyst ranked only once at least 10 different users watch it, users never named; a page and an RSS feed on the paid tier until the owner sets a tier
- [ ] X feed of the ranking (owner decisions first: which of ADR 0029's options a, b, or c, and whether to add a higher tier and at what price): posts filled in by plain code from a template the owner approves once, on a fixed schedule, original posts only, each with the notice and a link to our page; its own daily spending cap; owner setup (an X account and an X developer account); first post only after checkpoint CP12

## Phase 10 — Corporate events, safety events, deal dates, growth blockers, past catalysts, and the full API (owner request 2026-10-18; ADR 0030)

- [ ] Past catalysts page: each resolved catalyst with its outcome, source, and measured move (abnormal return on day 0, +1, and +5 against XBI, 30-day run-up, volume against its 20-day average); event-window statistics only, never a price series; free sees 90 days; needs the price license
- [ ] Corporate events in history and kept current, each from its filing with a quote: executive arrivals and departures (CEO, CFO, chief medical, scientific, and operating officers, president, board chair; 8-K Item 5.02 or 6-K); major financings (offerings, private placements, registered directs, at-the-market sales, convertible and other debt, royalty deals; "major" is at least 10% of market value, or at least $50 million where market value is not yet licensed, set in config); collaborations and deals (licences, co-development, options, asset sales; partners, upfront, milestones, royalties as disclosed, else "terms not disclosed")
- [ ] Upcoming deal dates as catalysts: a merger's expected closing window, shareholder vote, tender offer expiration, antitrust waiting-period end, and outside date; a collaboration's effective date and option deadlines; shown in a "Corporate events" section and filter, and as their own types in price statistics
- [ ] Growth blockers and their fixes ("Commercial milestones"; owner request 2026-10-18): a company statement naming what limits revenue or earnings growth, the fix (manufacturing capacity, a second supplier or contract manufacturer, licensing production out, a new country, reimbursement or a payer contract or payment scheme, a label or formulation change, sales-force growth), the product, the timeline (same date parser), the quote, and the label "company expectation"; from the company's own releases, filings (10-K and 10-Q risk factors and management's discussion, 8-K and 6-K exhibits), and presentations and transcripts it posts itself; every later mention updates its history (on track, moved, done, dropped), a slip can alert, silence shows "no update since", and a blocker with no timeline stays off the calendar
- [ ] Safety events and read-through to competitors: clinical holds and lifted holds, trials paused, stopped, or ended (with the stated reason), data monitoring committee recommendations, company-reported deaths or serious side effects, boxed warnings and safety label changes, recalls, FDA safety communications, and withdrawals, from company releases and filings, ClinicalTrials.gov status changes, and openFDA (news is a lead only); adverse-event report counts shown as unverified context, never a catalyst; a competitor (same indication, target, or mechanism, from the reference tables) whose abnormal return on day 0 or +1 passes a threshold (starting at twice its own typical daily abnormal move over 60 trading days, set in config) gets a "peer event" in its history with the original event, its move, and "same-day move; the cause is not established"; needs the price license for peer entries
- [ ] Both readers taught the new types: new prompt versions, gold-set entries for each type, both scored again before any of them publishes (checkpoint CP13); history back-filled from past filings over several weeks inside the daily caps
- [ ] Public REST API tier (moved from Later; about $50 a month until the owner decides): every published dataset with its sources, OpenAPI, versioned paths, cursor paging, ETags, API keys per account, rate limits and usage metering by plan, a daily file of changes for bulk use, never a price series; whether it shares a plan with ADR 0029's possible higher tier is decided together

## Phase 11 — Research reports from our own data (owner request 2026-10-18; ADR 0032)

- [ ] Report topics and data thresholds: a topic starts only when its sample has at least 200 resolved events and 30 in each compared group (set in config); topics are descriptive base rates such as slip rates by phase and size, price reactions to advisory votes, PDUFA outcomes, and complete response letters, how well company-confirmed dates hold, peer moves on safety events, insider purchases against outcomes, and our own scenario accuracy
- [ ] Report builder: a fixed analysis script per report computes every number (counts, medians, percentiles, confidence intervals, sample sizes, a naive baseline for anything forward-looking); the analyst role writes the text from that table and a test checks every number in it; a methods section, the data's date range, exclusions, and the "Research, not financial advice" notice; no buy or sell language or single price targets; Opus review of the methods (checkpoint CP14) and owner approval before each release; a fixed release schedule
- [ ] Access and sales (owner decides when this phase starts): included in the paid tier or a higher tier, sold one at a time through Stripe Checkout, or both; a free summary page per report with its question, method, one headline figure, and every source link; price-derived reports only after the price vendor confirms in writing that they may be shown and sold

## Later (unscheduled)

- [ ] Second model provider for verification
- [ ] Conference abstracts at scale; Slack and Teams apps (regulator sources outside the US moved to Phase 5)
- [ ] Estimated results-reporting window for each trial (owner request, 2026-10-17). A trial's primary completion date is when the last measurement is due, not when results come out. Show a second window for when results are likely to be reported, chosen in this order:
  1. The company's own guidance ("topline data in 1H27"), whenever it exists.
  2. Otherwise, the company's own history: for its past trials, the gap between primary completion (ClinicalTrials.gov) and the press release or filing that reported the results.
  3. Otherwise, a base rate for similar trials (phase, and later therapeutic area), for companies with no reporting history yet.

  The estimate carries the "inferred" date label and a tooltip saying which of the three it used, how many past trials it rests on, and the spread of their gaps. Guidance always replaces an estimate. Known limits to state in the tooltip: completion dates in the registry are often revised, and companies may take longer to report bad results, so historical gaps can run short.
